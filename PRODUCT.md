# Santh Digital

Local business digital growth and appointment platform.

**Thesis:** Turn local searches into measurable leads and confirmed appointments.

**First vertical:** Dental clinics in Hyderabad  
**First product:** Clinic website + online booking + lead capture + clinic dashboard  
**Stack:** Next.js, Hasura GraphQL, PostgreSQL  
**First business goal:** 3 paying clinics

This is not a website agency product. The north-star question for every clinic is:

> How much business did our digital system generate for you?

## MVP definition of done

A new patient can: reach the clinic site → choose a service → choose a doctor → pick a real slot → confirm.

The clinic can: see the appointment immediately → manage status → see the patient/lead → change availability.

Explicitly out of MVP: mobile app, EMR, payments, WhatsApp/SMS, website builder, marketplace.
